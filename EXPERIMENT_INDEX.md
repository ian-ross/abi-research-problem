# Experiment Index

## Active autonomous research policy

[`campaign-reports/abi-045-commissioned-autonomy-policy.md`](campaign-reports/abi-045-commissioned-autonomy-policy.md) records the transition from commissioning to the same operator-invoked autonomy model used by `../gvccs-research-problem`. Direct operator invocation of `autonomy-step` or `run-autonomous-iteration` is sufficient authority for the bounded work performed by the command. Ordinary research operations require no Backlog task, implementation plan, campaign authorization report, `campaign_resumed` event, separate per-step approval, clean/pushed Git gate, manual boundary refresh, lifecycle-count baseline, or manual GPU-idle attestation.

The active trusted Workspace Configuration remains controlling: at most 1,024 ABI-038-selected train and validation records per Dataset Source and Leakage-Safe Split, 12 epochs, batch size 4, concurrency one, a 3,600-second Docker training timeout, four `first_n` predictions, 25 million parameters, constant learning rate only, disabled early stopping, and pinned A100 GPU 0. Candidate source, manifests, and Agent handoffs cannot raise these values or replace provider/Harness ownership of data, sampling, loss, metrics, Artifact Filters, coordinate exclusion, resource placement, execution, or lifecycle behavior. If the operator later activates different trusted limits, invoking the command is sufficient authority to use them.

An explicit `campaign_paused` state and Harness stop/failure conditions still stop autonomous iteration. Caller disconnect, stale state, or a failed step never authorizes duplicate or replacement execution. Capability Requests and Campaign Reports may deliberately return control to the operator. Promotion, deployment, and production claims remain operator decisions; ordinary bounded research does not wait for a separate authorization ceremony.

ABI-034 through ABI-044 are historical commissioning records. Their task-specific gates, one-step approvals, hard stops, and requirements for later separate authorization applied to those operations at the time and are prospectively superseded; their scientific evidence and trusted-boundary findings remain valid. In particular, [`campaign-reports/abi-043-first-promoted-architecture-resource-pilot.md`](campaign-reports/abi-043-first-promoted-architecture-resource-pilot.md) and [`campaign-reports/abi-044-first-representative-architecture-scout.md`](campaign-reports/abi-044-first-representative-architecture-scout.md) document the completed DeepLabV3+ resource pilot and representative scout, not current launch gates.
